Photo tips
  • Handle the stamp with tongs. Skin oils stain paper and gum.
  • Lay it flat on a plain dark card. Edges are found most reliably against black.
  • Use soft daylight from one side. Tilt the phone slightly if you see a bright spot.
  • Hold the phone flat above the stamp, about a hand's width away. Use 2x zoom if it will not focus.
  • For a whole album or stockbook page, choose Album page. Every stamp on it is found and sorted in one go, and you can then identify the promising ones on their own.
